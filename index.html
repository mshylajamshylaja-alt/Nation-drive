DATA SOURCES & REFERENCES

SOURCES & METHODOLOGY

Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH), Government of India — Road Accidents in India annual reports and State/UT tables.
Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India — State/UT-wise road accident fatality information.
Official Government road-safety / transport reporting used to cross-check the State/UT series through 2024.
Project scope displayed: 2016–2026; state-level values are populated only where officially verified.
REPORTING NOTES

2016–2017 state rows follow the MoRTH 2020 state table; later national totals were revised in subsequent reporting, so historical state sums may differ from the latest national headline totals. 2018 state values are from the official 2024 PIB/MoRTH annexure. 2022–2024 state values are from the official Rajya Sabha/MoRTH answer dated 18 Mar 2026. 2025 is national-only and 2026 is provisional/YTD. Jammu & Kashmir / Ladakh and Dadra & Nagar Haveli / Daman & Diu have administrative/reporting changes across the period. Missing values are shown as NA rather than invented.
